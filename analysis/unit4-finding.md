# Unit 4: Leadership finding

The renter no-show rate is 6%, or 12 of 200 viewings. Property rates range
up to 18.75% among properties with viewings. This counts recorded
renter no-shows; other cancellations and disputes are outside this measure.

I would investigate property 2 first, then property 3.

| Property ID | No-shows / viewings | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2 | 3 / 16 | 18.75% |
| 3 | 2 / 13 | 15.38% |

Together, they account for 41.67% of failures but only 14.5% of viewings.
Only property 2 meets our 15-viewing minimum, so I would be cautious about
drawing conclusions from property 3's rate.

The regional property operations manager should review reminders,
cancellation reasons, and repeat renters before deciding what to change.
